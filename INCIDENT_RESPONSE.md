# Incident Response Plan

## Goals
- Contain security incidents quickly.
- Protect user data and privacy.
- Communicate clearly and transparently.

## Severity Levels
- Critical: active exploitation, data compromise, or RCE.
- High: major data exposure risk or privilege escalation.
- Medium: limited exposure or non-critical bypass.
- Low: best-practice improvements or minor bugs.

## Process
1. Triage within 24 hours and assign an owner.
2. Reproduce and confirm scope.
3. Contain and mitigate (release hotfix if needed).
4. Communicate impact and remediation timeline.
5. Publish a postmortem with preventive actions.
